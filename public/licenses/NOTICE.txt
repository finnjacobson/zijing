字境 (Zijing): third-party data and licenses
=============================================

The files under data/ were generated by scripts/build-data.mjs, which downloads, merges, MODIFIES
and re-shards the datasets below into one JSON file per character. They are not the original files.
The license texts in this directory apply as follows.

data/s/*.json  (stroke outlines and medians)
  Make Me a Hanzi graphics.txt, derived from Arphic PL KaitiM GB and Arphic PL UKai.
  Copyright (C) 1999 Arphic Technology Co., Ltd.  License: ARPHIC-PUBLIC-LICENSE.txt
  https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi

data/t/*.json  (Taiwan-standard stroke outlines)
  AnimCJK graphicsZhHant.txt, Copyright 2016-2026 FM&SH, derived from Arphic PL KaitiM Big5/GB.
  License: ARPHIC-PUBLIC-LICENSE.txt
  https://github.com/parsimonhi/animCJK

data/c/*.json  (character pages) contains material from:
  - Make Me a Hanzi dictionary.txt (decomposition, radical, etymology)
      License: LGPL-3.0-makemeahanzi.txt (which incorporates GPL-3.0.txt)
  - CC-CEDICT (definitions, pinyin, words), © MDBG and CC-CEDICT contributors
      License: CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt. These derived files are shared under the same license.
      https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary?page=cc-cedict
  - Unicode Unihan Database 18.0 (readings, strokes, radicals, variants), © Unicode, Inc.
      License: UNICODE-LICENSE.txt. The data has been modified and reformatted.
  - SUBTLEX-CH word and character frequencies, used for ranking. Free for research and
      non-commercial use. Cite: Cai, Q., & Brysbaert, M. (2010). SUBTLEX-CH: Chinese word and
      character frequencies based on film subtitles. PLoS ONE, 5(6), e10729.
  - 說文解字 entries from shuowenjiezi/shuowen. License: APACHE-2.0-shuowen.txt
      https://github.com/shuowenjiezi/shuowen  (the classical text itself is public domain)
  - Wikimedia Commons Ancient Chinese Characters Project: file names/paths only (public domain / CC0)

data/w/*.json  (word lists for segmentation and glosses)
  CC-CEDICT (CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt) and SUBTLEX-CH frequencies (see above).

data/z/*.json  (small-seal outlines)
  Kaiyuan Small Seal glyphs, © Kaiyuan Small Seal Font Contributors.
  License: OFL-1.1-Kaiyuan-Small-Seal.txt (SIL Open Font License 1.1)
  https://github.com/frankslin/kaiyuan-small-seal-font

  License: CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt
